Relson
Gracie

Self-defenseSeminars

Relson Gracie is the second oldest son of Grandmaster Helio Gracie, the man credited with creating what is now known as Gracie Jiu-Jitsu. Relson has trained since he was two and began competing at 10. He was the Brazilian National Champion for 22 consecutive years in both his weight class and the open division, earning the nickname “Campeão,” Portuguese for champion. In 1988 he moved to Hawaii, bringing his art with him. He still teaches all over the world and visits Charleston often for seminars, private lessons and competitions. Long retired from competition, he trains, like his father, for self-defense and personal advancement.

03 Black belt · Relson Gracie
Instructor

Jerry
Brewer

CompetitionCombatives

Jerry Brewer is a certified Gracie Jiu-Jitsu black belt under Grandmaster Relson Gracie. He has trained since 1998 and is credited with bringing Gracie Jiu-Jitsu to Charleston. He won gold in the advanced heavyweight division of the 2004 SC State Grappling Championship, took silver in the heavyweight division of the 2003 Arnold Gracie World Championships, and led his team to six consecutive state championships. A shoulder injury ended his competition career in 2006. His proudest accomplishment is teaching, and he is certified as a Level 3 Modern Army Combatives Instructor, a level few civilians reach.

James is our resident Adult BJJ and Striking Fundamentals coach. He has trained Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and mixed martial arts since 2007, received his black belt from Master Relson Gracie in 2022, and has helped many students reach their own promotions since. He holds a 5-2 amateur MMA record, with three wins by submission. Students love how he teaches advanced techniques in a beginner-friendly way and puts the striking-shy at ease. His eldest started jiu jitsu here and joins his dad's striking classes every week.

05 Purple belt · Relson Gracie
Instructor · Youth coach

Sebastian
Seagraves

YouthMMA

Sebastian is our resident Youth coach and assistant wrestling and striking coach, and he coaches Fundamentals and Adult BJJ as scheduling allows. A purple belt under Relson Gracie, Mike Key, Pat McGuigan and James Foy, he trains here most days of the week. He started jiu jitsu at Gracie Charleston in 2016 and earned his blue belt in 2018, then served four years in the US Marine Corps Infantry, where he began fighting MMA. He came home in 2022 and earned his purple belt that July. He is an active MMA fighter with a 5-1-0 record (as of February 2024), mostly first-round finishes.
